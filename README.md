# 📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The following KPIs were calculated during the analysis:

### ✅ Total Sales
Total revenue generated from all orders.

### ✅ Total Profit
Overall profit earned from sales.

### ✅ Total Orders
Total number of unique customer orders.

### ✅ Total Customers
Total number of unique customers.

### ✅ Average Order Value
Average revenue generated per order.

Formula:

```
Average Order Value = Total Sales / Total Orders
```

# 📊 Deep-Dive Analysis

The following analyses were performed:

## 1. Monthly Sales Trend

- Examined monthly sales performance.
- Identified seasonal trends.
- Observed growth and decline in sales over time.

---

## 2. Region-wise Sales Analysis

Compared sales across different regions to identify:

- Highest revenue region
- Lowest revenue region
- Regional business performance

---

## 3. Category-wise Sales Analysis

Analyzed product categories to determine:

- Best-performing category
- Least-performing category
- Category contribution to revenue

---

## 4. Top Products Analysis

Identified the Top 10 products based on total sales.

This helps businesses understand:

- Best-selling products
- Customer preferences
- Inventory planning

---

## 5. Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)

Customers were segmented using:

- Recency
- Frequency
- Monetary Value

Customer groups:

- Loyal Customers
- Regular Customers
- Inactive Customers

This segmentation helps businesses improve customer retention and marketing strategies.

# 📈 Business Insights

The analysis revealed several valuable business insights:

- Monthly sales showed clear seasonal trends.
- Certain regions consistently generated higher revenue.
- Some product categories contributed significantly more profit than others.
- A small percentage of products generated a large share of total sales.
- Loyal customers accounted for a major portion of revenue.
- Customer segmentation highlighted opportunities for targeted marketing campaigns.
- Business decisions can be improved by focusing on high-performing regions and customer groups.
